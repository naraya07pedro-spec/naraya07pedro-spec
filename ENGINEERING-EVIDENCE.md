## Evidence and release boundary

- **Archived metrics stay archived.** The Agent Runtime v2 report's `261 tests / 90.01% coverage` belongs to its [preserved v2 evidence](https://github.com/naraya07pedro-spec/agent-runtime-python/tree/f292f3cdb5d6daf5650e495898dbe8f8a710ea75/evidence/v2), not automatically to the `main` SHA above. Console's earlier 58-test evidence similarly describes its linked revision only.
- **Client outcomes are not automated-test results.** [CLIENT-WORK.md](CLIENT-WORK.md) labels two engagements as owner-described scope without independently publishable client-origin handoff or outcome metrics. Reference-repo tests cannot validate their customer ROI.
- **Portfolio release remains separate.** At this snapshot, [Portfolio main CI](https://github.com/naraya07pedro-spec/evan-naraya-portfolio/actions/runs/43350725584) and [release eligibility](https://github.com/naraya07pedro-spec/evan-naraya-portfolio/actions/runs/43350725502) were blocked. The [security/QA remediation PR #22](https://github.com/naraya07pedro-spec/evan-naraya-portfolio/pull/22) was still review-only. A green security subjob does not waive browser, visual, or **owner-approved CV hash** release gates.
- **Data provenance:** no customer secrets, identifiable private lead lists, commercial agreement details or unapproved client implementation artifacts are published in this reviewer index. Source and tests listed here are public, synthetic references.

## Maintenance rule

Whenever a highlighted flagship advances, compare the new **default-branch SHA**, exact-head **CI run and job conclusions**, source/test paths and limitations before changing this table. A passing check proves only its executed scope, not every untested failure mode. Do not turn the contribution graph, stale badges or self-reported commercial outcomes into engineering evidence.
